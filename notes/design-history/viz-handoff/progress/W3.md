## Risks
- Finalizing a large capture (tens of MB) runs the bitwise CRC and CSV formatting on the capture thread; on a Pi this may take seconds, during which the hub ring (16384) fills and drops (counted, reported). Typical captures are a few MB.
- Worst-case memory while building one bundle is about 3 x max_mb (records, CSV text, ZIP), plus `keep` finished bundles. Documented.
- Event text is truncated to 119 bytes by the `DiagEvent` contract.
- The synthetic-rig System test only checks the tap and ordering; placed rows with measured times were not reached within its 0.8 s window (the rig holds for bias calibration first).
