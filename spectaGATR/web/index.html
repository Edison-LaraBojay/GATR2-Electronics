Conventions

  • Origin (white ring): inside bottom-left corner of the field diagram.
  • +X right on the diagram, +Y up on the diagram, +Z up. Overhead view shows the diagram as drawn.
  • Heading: robot forward measured from +X, counterclockwise positive. 0 faces +X (right), +90 faces +Y (up). Shown in degrees.
  • Robot: +x forward (red arrow, white face), +y left (green arrow, green side), +z up (blue arrow). Box drawn about the pose origin.
  • Roll about robot +x: positive lifts the left side.
  • Pitch about robot +y: positive tips the nose down.
  • Rotation robot to field: R = Rz(yaw) Ry(pitch) Rx(roll). Attitude only rotates the box; it never raises it.
  • Trail: received positions; it breaks on a new session, a pose reset (epoch), a reconnect, or a gap longer than the stale time.
  • Receive age: time since the receiver accepted the shown pose (laptop clock). It is not end-to-end latency.
